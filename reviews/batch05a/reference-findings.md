# Independent references and preflight

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Sources are comparison evidence only, not deliverable texture assets. Review standard: review_standard_revision/docs/asset-standard.md.

## Stool
https://www.pinefactory.jp/product.php?code=stool-s-na
Official maker specifies 280 x 280 x 450 mm; pine, oil finishing; separately assembled seat, legs and stretchers. Manufacturer photos independently inspected: square splayed legs, two assembled side frames, round staggered stretchers, seat underside cleats and bolt holes. Source says stretchers are not climbing steps. Original proposed 300 mm seat is deliberately distinct. Reviewer requested flat-cut feet, coplanar felt soles, contacting upper rails/seat cleats instead of unsupported dowel-leg/tenon construction. This is structural plausibility, not load certification.
Direct seat photo independently retrieved: https://www.pinefactory.jp/upload/images/dining/stool/s-na/stool_s001_s.jpg
Full photo: https://www.pinefactory.jp/upload/images/dining/stool/s-na/stool-s01_s.jpg
Assembly photo inspected from maker download; URL pending provenance.

## Clock
https://www.seiko-clock.co.jp/product-personal/wall_clock/standard/qxa709b.html
Official product: 368 mm diameter, 43 mm depth, painted plastic frame, glass front, one AA cell. Original design may use 300 mm diameter. Linked https://www.seiko-clock.co.jp/product-personal/up_files/AKX-055P.pdf independently downloaded and page 2 visually inspected: rear movement enclosure, horizontal AA bay and setting knob. Manufacturer rear photo inspected: upper hanging slit with surrounding recessed cavity, mechanism behind central spindle, outer rear perimeter against wall. Reviewer requested actual open keyhole/slit and head recess, layered rotating hands with face/glass clearance, wall attachment use test.
Rear/side photo direct URLs pending provenance.

## Bucket
https://www.risu-kogyo.co.jp/protools/pdf/catalog_protools.pdf
Independently downloaded official RISU industrial catalogue and inspected bucket spread: Rabucket 8L/14L have metal bails and central plastic grip, plastic tapered bodies. Table identifies PE/EVA and 8L diameter 280 mm x 217 mm height.
https://store.shopping.yahoo.co.jp/risu-onlineshop/13030-7.html
RISU official storefront explicitly identifies 8L body PE/EVA, handle PP/stainless steel, 280 mm diameter and 217 mm height. Not the all-PP Belc or GK assumed from appearance.
Maker's Rabucket photo independently visually inspected: raised pierced ears above upper rim; wire bail ends pass through ear holes then turn back as hooks; center grip; small pouring notch. Reviewer requires that coherent construction rather than mixing a bolted steel pail pivot. Exact hook fit and body wall thickness remain original geometric estimates requiring saved-file tests.

Bucket specific official image https://www.risu.co.jp/group/search/file/file0030/t010000002972.jpg and drawing https://www.risu.co.jp/group/search/cad/BR8.pdf inspected. Drawing confirms 280 mm rim outer diameter, 217 mm vessel height, 235 mm including ears, 187.7 mm bottom diameter and 301 mm pouring-lip span. Drawing itself identifies these as standard/reference dimensions, not guaranteed dimensions. Proposed wall/base thickness and bail dimensions remain estimates.
