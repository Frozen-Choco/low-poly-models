# Candidate scope: red apple only

Bread and onigiri described below are preserved experimental history, held after independent visual review and excluded from the release manifest and context.

# Design and provenance

These are original artist-built generic food studies, not measured scans or exact commercial reproductions. Plausible dimensions are chosen for tabletop dioramas.

- Red apple: gently lobed asymmetric skin, depressed stem cavity, curved short woody stalk, dried underside calyx. Packed original skin pixels include mottling, fine red streaks and lenticels.
- Shokupan: soft rounded-square slices, thin golden crust, ivory crumb with irregular pores. Four closely spaced upright slices form the short sliced loaf; one additional slice rests flat at its side. Components are independently editable.
- Onigiri: hand-pressed rounded triangular rice mass, individual short-grain ellipsoids on its exterior, thin wrinkled seaweed sheet wrapped continuously under the base. Nori is matte dark green, with generated fiber variation and real shell thickness.

No third-party texture or geometry files. Procedural textures are packed into Blender and embedded as images in GLB; no unsupported procedural shader networks are needed by consumers. Grain geometry is retained for portable silhouette detail. This is intentionally a higher-detail food asset than the preceding household props.

Known scope: no cut apple interior, bread bag, plate, filling cross-section, brands, or labels. These are visual props, not nutrition or food-safety references. Structural tests do not substitute for independent visual review.
