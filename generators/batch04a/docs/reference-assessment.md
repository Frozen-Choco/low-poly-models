# Real-object reference assessment

Checked 2026-10-16. These are generic diorama models, not scans or replicas. All texture pixels and geometry are original. Reference imagery is viewed for construction and anatomy only; none is embedded or redistributed in assets.

## Whole red apple

- University of Maryland Extension, stem-end and calyx-end anatomy (labeled calyx photo directly viewed; surrounding damage is not reproduced): https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/managing-apple-growing-conditions-prevent-fruit-cracking
- Aomori Apple Association official Fuji photograph was directly viewed: https://aomori-ringo.or.jp/starcut/variety.html (image: https://aomori-ringo.or.jp/starcut/img/cut/38-fuji.png). It shows the curved stem, depressed stem end, red mottling/lenticels and rounded lower support. The inaccessible earlier variety PDF is not used as visual evidence. The model is a generic apple rather than a cultivar replica.
- Intended correspondence: woody stalk rises from stem-end depression; opposite pole has a recessed dried calyx. Surrounding lower fruit lobes support the apple rather than the stalk or calyx. Uneven red coloring and lenticels represent ordinary variation, not disease or the cracked fruit in the anatomy reference.
- Artist-inferred: exact cultivar, 84mm-scale width, shape asymmetry, calyx depth, stem bend, skin pattern and wax-like roughness. This is not labeled as a faithful Fuji specimen. The calyx must remain visible in underside inspection; an earlier buried version was rejected.

## Held: Sliced shokupan

- Pasco official hand-made loaf and slicing example: https://www.pascoshop.com/Page/LP/choujukuotoriyose/
- Preparation and flat-top/open-top form comparison: https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-milk-bread-shokupan/
- Intended correspondence: a rounded-square, flat-top tin-loaf form; golden exterior crust surrounds pale cut crumb. Four contiguous upright slices include an outer rear heel. The separate slice lies on its broad cut face beside the loaf, with real thickness and perimeter crust. This is one composite asset, not five separate counted products.
- Artist-inferred: 114mm-scale loaf width, 21.5mm upright slices, 19mm separate slice, pore distribution, crust thickness and color. No manufacturer dimensions or branded product matching claimed. Food pores are lower-contrast warm cavities, not dark inclusions; an earlier excessively dark pore version was rejected.

## Held: Nori-wrapped onigiri

- Kikkoman official preparation: https://www.kikkoman.com/en/cookbook/washoku/onigiri.html
- Additional actual food photo: https://www.justonecookbook.com/onigiri-rice-balls/
- Kikkoman describes gently shaping sticky japonica rice into a triangle, cutting nori into strips, then pressing a strip onto the lower portion. The model chooses this strip-wrap style rather than combining it with the broader side wrap shown in the additional photo.
- Intended correspondence: rice is a cohesive rounded triangle with tightly packed plump grains; a thin dark-green nori strip hugs front, lower rounded corners, base and back. It has small wrinkles and edge variation. Grains beneath the strip are excluded to prevent visible punctures. The sheet is not a thick black structural slab.
- Artist-inferred: 87mm-scale width, 49mm depth, 76mm height, grain distribution, exact strip width/thickness and texture. These are not recipe-derived exact physical dimensions. No filling is exposed or claimed. Sparse grains over a smooth core were rejected; the next focused correction is subject to independent approval or hold.

## Acceptance boundary

Food varies naturally. Reference agreement does not imply photogrammetric accuracy. The independent reviewer must compare actual GLB and Blend outputs, support contact, cut-face/heel/calyx relationships, nori fit and rice surface, under multiple lights and at close range. Structural validity alone is insufficient. Any asset still reading as an artificial stand-in is held rather than counted as complete.

## Final candidate scope

Only whole_red_apple is considered for release. Independent review rejected the bread as insufficiently aerated/too uniformly smooth and the onigiri as angular grain clusters over a smooth core with overly flat nori. These two experiments are held without further parameter iterations. Their files, metadata and earlier contexts are not release candidates. Final contexts contain the apple alone.
