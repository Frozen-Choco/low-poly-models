# Original office props: evidence and scope
Research date: 2026-10-16. These are generic diorama assets, not replicas, fabrication plans, or engineering-certified mechanisms. External references were read; no reference images were incorporated as textures.

## Corrugated carton
Primary reference: FEFCO Code, 12th edition, https://www.fefco.org/sites/default/files/documents/FEFCO%20Code_WEBprotected.pdf (0201 slotted-box construction and manufacturer glue joint).
Verified relationships: one folded wall blank with manufacturer joint; short flaps fold inside long flaps; long closure flaps meet at center; tape runs along the center seam and turns down the end walls. The bottom is the vertical mirror of the top closure. Model includes interior glue tab, 3 mm wall/flap thickness and separate closure layers. Open-flap inspection pose will expose overlap.
Estimated: 320 x 240 x 206 mm size, 48 mm gummed tape width, 3 mm board caliper. Fiber albedo is original isotropic noise, not wood grain. Hidden corrugation is represented by board thickness rather than individual flutes.

## Stapler
Primary reference: Rapid Classic K2+ https://www.rapid.com/en-lv/products/stapling--punching/desktop-staplers/rapid-classic-k2-fullstrip-stapler/ (steel desktop construction, adjustable anvil, stapling and pinning use). This reference supports the general metal desktop construction, not the dimensions of this original smaller prop.
Verified relationships modeled: transverse rear hinge, lower base and anvil, separate U-channel magazine, pressure cover, staple crowns and legs, driver located above the clinching channels. Hand pressure acts on the upper arm; paper enters between the magazine nose and base. Functional test rotates the upper assembly around its hinge to check closing clearance and the driver projection.
Estimated: 158 mm overall length, 47 mm width, body curvature, exact mechanism details. Anvil is shown in a fixed stapling orientation. The asset is a static unrigged prop: return spring/pusher hidden inside the shell are omitted; no claim of animated staple feed. Exposed parts and paper path must remain physically coherent despite this simplification.

## Clipboard
Primary reference: Saunders recycled hardboard clipboard https://saunders-usa.com/products/recycled-hardboard-clipboard-legal-8-5-x-14-05613 (page title identifies Letter Size 05612; URL is misleading). Hardboard writing surface and spring clip function confirmed.
Related construction reference: Saunders low-profile steel clip with rubber grips https://saunders-usa.com/products/aluminum-clipboard-8-5-x-12-21517 . This is explicitly a different board material. The visible wire-jaw, two rubber grips, central finger-lift offset and rear rolled assembly inform our original hardboard+clip combination, not a claimed product replica.
Verified model relationships: board supports full A4 paper extent; paper ends before fixed rivets and backplate; two rubber corner pads contact the top sheet; a wire jaw with central finger-lift offset connects to the transverse rolled hinge. Two coaxial spring coils model the closing-force arrangement. No hanging loop is claimed.
Estimated: 235 x 340 x 3.2 mm board, five 0.105 mm paper sheets separated by small visualization gaps, wire path, coil dimensions and rear pin anchorage. The manufacturer photograph supports the visible wire-jaw/two-pad/rolled-hinge arrangement; exact unseen spring construction is an inference. No load or fatigue simulation claimed.

## Corrections before independent review
- Mirrored bottom closure layers so tape lies on the exposed underside; corner tape overlaps rather than floating. Long wall upper folds now contact the top flaps.
- Aligned stapler driver and anvil along the same Y coordinate; packed 12.8 mm staple row on a support rail with clearance for 6 mm legs.
- Moved clipboard paper clear of fixed rivets, supported the entire A4 extent, and replaced an unsupported bowed-sheet design with source-visible sprung wire topology.

Pose scope: the stapler raised-arm view rotates the complete upper assembly and is not a full magazine-refill demonstration. The separate +2.5 degree press pose measures driver/anvil projection and lower-mechanism/base clearance. Clipboard open-jaw pose lifts the wire jaw and rubber pads around the rear transverse hinge. Carton open-flap pose exposes the inner end flaps after top tape is hidden as if removed.
