### Flat-panel shading regression and future design check
Revision6 (refrigerator only) corrects cabinet planar-face shading. The inherited Boolean helper used a name-based special-case list, and unrecognized objects marked vertical planes smooth while relying on Weighted Normal. A renamed/new cabinet therefore retained smooth normals across large Boolean-cut planes and adjacent bevels, producing wide triangular gradients at cavity corners in the canonical open image. The remedy is explicit per-model face classification after the last Boolean: axis-parallel cabinet planes flat, small curved/beveled faces smooth. Geometry positions, materials, hinges and frozen photography settings stay unchanged.

For every future box-like appliance, check broad Boolean-cut front/side planes in an early open-state oblique close-up, inspect use_smooth and split normals, and compare a normals-only diagnostic before final photography. Do not rely on inherited object-name allowlists or correct lighting/exposure to hide a shading defect. Retain smooth normals for actually curved surfaces and narrow bevels. The complete revision5 source/model and640px A/B evidence are retained locally for independent delta verification.

Revision7 closes a second export-specific regression: merely flipping smooth flags before BMesh cleanup left stale custom corner-normal data and produced a new triangular gradient on the GLB rear panel. After all cleanup and UV work, the final generator now clears cabinet custom normals, regenerates weighted normals for the bevels, and explicitly assigns the geometric face normal to each broad planar corner. All polygons retain the smooth flag to carry explicit glTF corner normals consistently; planar faces nevertheless have flat normals. GLB rear-panel normal Y components improved from roughly0.69–0.99 to≥0.999993. Geometry/UV/material delta review is independent. The prevention check must inspect the exported GLB, not only the in-memory Blend preview, and must occur after all topology writing operations.

### Punched-cylinder shading correction
Washing-machine revision7 changes only the perforated basket normals after final cleanup/UV. Smoothing across both cylindrical wall faces and thickness end faces made the otherwise cylindrical wall appear dented around every opening. Normals are now analytic radial directions for the inner/outer cylinder and geometric face normals for the punched-edge thickness surfaces. An independent A/B confirmed disappearance of the false rectangular dimples with true holes retained. Future perforated-shell gates must include a640px or larger oblique interior diagnostic at32–64 samples before canonical photography, not only small/noisy all-angle previews; validate exported normals at wall/edge transitions separately.
