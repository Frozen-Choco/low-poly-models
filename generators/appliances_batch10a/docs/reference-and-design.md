### Final exposed-coplanar regression prevention
The initial high-quality television front showed tiny black fragments at four corners where separate bezel members overlapped on the same face. This was detected before completing the full photo set. Television revision6 uses a single continuous rounded rectangular bezel with a real aperture. Vacuum revision9 likewise uses a single continuous exhaust frame and recessed louver front faces. A trial union of separately beveled bars created degenerate micro-triangles and was rejected by saved-format validation; the final rings are built from one outer solid and one aperture and have zero degenerate triangles in both saved formats. Exposed same-plane overlap is now reviewed separately from legitimate concealed assembly engagement.
