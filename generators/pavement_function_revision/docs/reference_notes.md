# Official references and generic scope

The original batch03b functional approval was withdrawn. This revision's geometry and evidence are described in functional_revision.md; that document supersedes earlier construction assumptions.

- Manhole slip relief: https://hinodesuido.co.jp/cast_line/products/single-01_manhole.html . Original generic pattern, 604 mm cover / 720 mm frame. No municipality artwork, load rating, seal, locking or flood restraint claimed.
- Opposed lifting and key mechanisms: Kaneso opening note and actual catalog drawings, listed in functional_revision.md. Generic true through-slots and review-only cross-foot keys, not a claim of compatibility with a particular manufacturer's handle.
- B-type curb: https://kawamoto-con.jp/product/p0012/ and https://www.toyo-con.co.jp/toyodb/catalogue.php?f=95be6a8eb9de4551277e69fd1f5a0b90 . Nominal 600 mm length, 180/205 mm widths, 250 mm height, 30 mm shoulder. Full-depth standalone block; context is visual placement only and omits curb bedding/drainage design.
- Bollard: https://www.sunpole.co.jp/product-detail/?no=407 and actual FPA-8B-F00 drawing. Generic external diameter/height class only; this flange, anchor pitch and cap intentionally differ and are documented estimates. Continuous buried shanks and a review-only inferred bonded-concrete attachment replace disconnected hardware.

All asset textures are original seeded locally generated raster basecolor, roughness and tangent normal maps, packed in Blend and embedded in GLB. No reference photography, artwork or third-party texture is embedded. Manufacturer PDFs stored during research are not included in the delivery manifest. Review-only key/support geometry is separate from asset GLBs. These are visual models, not manufacturing or installation instructions.
