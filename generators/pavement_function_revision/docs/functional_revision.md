# Functional-reference revision

This replaces the withdrawn batch03b candidate. Original files are preserved separately. These are visual diorama assets, not manufacturing, structural or safety-certified designs.

## Manhole
Official Kaneso catalog page 713 (PDF page 43) shows A-type cross-foot and B-type hook lifting handles; the official two-handle opening note requires opposed engagement and warns of single-sided interference. The model is a generic flat-seat cover, not a Kaneso MKHY or Hinode V-seat replica. Its original 44×18 mm slots are now truly open through the 32 mm plate. Review-only 30×6 mm cross-feet insert along X, descend clear of the underside, rotate 90 degrees, and engage the plate from below. Two handles move together. This is an inferred, mechanically coherent cross-key interface rather than a claimed exact manufacturer's handle compatibility.

- Plate underside and ledge bearing top both Z=0.047 m; no penetration.
- Ledge inner radius 0.285 m. Shortened rotated ribs reach about 0.2801 m maximum radius, leaving about 4.9 mm clearance.
- Cover radius 0.302 m versus frame inner radius 0.307 m leaves 5 mm radial lift clearance.
- Insertion foot 30×6 mm fits 44×18 mm slot with 7 mm end and 6 mm side clearance.
- Turn occurs with foot top Z=0.037 m, 10 mm below the underside.
- Turned foot spans 30 mm across the 18 mm slot, giving 6 mm nominal bearing overlap on each side. Engaged foot top Z=0.047 m.
- The whole cover, ribs and raised grip lift vertically together; no hinge, lock, seal, road load rating or flood restraint is claimed.
- Installed translation remains -0.080 m: frame top road-flush, plate top -1 mm, grip +3 mm.

## Bollard
The actual Sunpole FPA-8B-F00 drawing was inspected. Its 150×150×6 mm flange, four Ø13 mm bores at 100 mm pitch and Ø20 mm drain differ from this deliberately generic 180×180×10 mm / 128 mm pitch flange. Those departures are explicitly inferred; only the 76.3 mm diameter / 850 mm visible height class is reference-informed.

This revision drills four Ø13 mm flange holes and adds continuous Ø10 mm shanks from Z=-0.100 to +0.026 m. Bored washers rest on the flange; bored hexagonal nuts rest on washers. Nut/shank interfaces are simplified nominal thread envelopes, not modeled helical threads. Hole-to-shank radial clearance is 1.5 mm; washer bore clearance is 0.5 mm. The mounting-plane origin is Z=0, with visible height 850 mm and total mesh bounds 950 mm. Buried rods are part of the asset and must be embedded in placement. Internal pipe wall/drain details are omitted; no drainage function is claimed.

Review-only installation uses a concrete support block and indicative bonded anchor zones, with cutaway evidence. Its 100 mm embedment is an artistic assumed dimension, not a maker anchor specification or engineered foundation design. Site surface is flush with flange underside. The solid support substitutes for the paver directly under the bollard; it is not attached only to a loose paving tile. Loads, concrete strength, reinforcement, adhesive specification and substrate design are not certified.

## Curb
Geometry is unchanged. Rounded/battered -Y faces the road, +Y the sidewalk. The block is full-depth; the visual mount-up-like context embeds 140 mm and exposes 110 mm, with 5 mm end joints. These installation dimensions are artistic assumptions. Curb bedding/foundation and drainage crossfall, gutter and outlet are intentionally omitted, so the context is only a visual placement aid, not a functionally complete installation demonstration. No drainage or foundation adequacy claim is made.
