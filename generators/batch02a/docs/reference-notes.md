# Reference basis and design choices

Checked 2026-10-15. These sources establish product context, not exact model dimensions or conformity. All geometry and finish images are original.

## Streetlight

https://www.iwasaki.co.jp/lighting/road_tunnel/anticrime/

The manufacturer offers compact LED street/security luminaires for Japanese local-road settings. Its calculation example uses 5 m mounting height, not a universal product height. This original 4 m small-street fixture uses a tapered galvanized mast, short outreach, sealed pale-grey luminaire, underside optic cover, service hatch and base anchors. The 4 m height and housing dimensions are design estimates for diorama use, not manufacturer specifications. A commercial photometric model is not included.

## Park bench

https://townscape.kotobuki.co.jp/product/bench/
https://townscape.kotobuki.co.jp/catalogs/images/KTB-42.pdf

The manufacturer shows wooden/composite slat benches and metal supporting structures in Japanese parks and streets; its catalog includes 1,800 mm class benches. This original bench uses a 1.8 m slat length, approximately 0.45 m seat height, separate timber slats, powder-coated rails and legs, fixing heads and isolation pads. It is an unbranded generic construction rather than a replica of a particular catalog model. Timber grain is a compact original approximation; species is deliberately unspecified.

## Traffic cone

https://www.sanko-kk.co.jp/products/safety/corn/

The manufacturer distinguishes standard, flexible, heavy/weighted, and top-hole cone families. This original model combines generic flexible red shell, two pale bands, top opening and a heavy dark base. The solid ballast base makes this visual prop non-stackable; ordinary open-bottom stacking is not modeled. Height 0.7 m and base width 0.38 m are approximate common-scale design choices. The reflective bands use standard PBR color/roughness and do not simulate optical retroreflection. No road-safety certification is implied.

## Material intent

- Timber: low-contrast elongated original grain, moderate surface roughness, no metallic response
- Powder coat: opaque dielectric coating, moderate-to-high roughness, clean manufactured edge radii
- Galvanized metal: neutral original low-contrast finish image, muted metallic response and high roughness
- PVC/polycarbonate: dielectric response, material-appropriate gloss without chrome
- EPDM/ballast rubber: dark, rough, low-reflectance finish

Each texture is packed in the Blend and embedded in the exported GLB. Ground slabs in context images are evidence staging only.
