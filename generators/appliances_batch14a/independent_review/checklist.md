# Independent toaster and fan acceptance checklist

Approval pins exact saved bytes and does not authorize publication. Production files are read-only to the reviewer.

1. Independently check manufacturer primary evidence for dimensions, visible materials, structural connections and mechanisms. Record reference-specific facts separately from generic design choices.
2. Toaster: finite casing and two open bread-slot cavity walls, grounded feet, carriage/tray/heater support and plausible lever/control attachment. Inspect lever/carriage and crumb-tray travel, clearances and concealed engagements. No heat or electrical certification claim.
3. Fan: grounded base, mechanically connected stem, tilt/swivel joints and motor, attached blades/hub and guard with actual thickness and clearance. Check intended movement ranges and guard separation; no operational or safety certification claim.
4. Audit saved Blend and GLB independently: finite coordinates, real metric bounds, outward closed manifold parts, no duplicate/degenerate faces, support graph, materials and normals. Compare corresponding geometry bidirectionally.
5. Direct unoriented-plane grouping and projected clipping must cover fully coplanar faces, shared-vertex and same-object cases. Classify every overlap and nonadjacent intersection category; reject exposed overlaps, weak support and unexplained floating parts.
6. Original-resolution diagnostic views in both formats must pass before high-quality canonical photography is authorized. Inspect all functional/detail views and sampled poses. No lighting changes to hide failures.
7. Canonical renderer/spec are frozen:1200px,512sample ceiling,0.005adaptive threshold,denoising off. Individually inspect all22standard final views at original resolution, plus any justified mechanism views. Verify immutable provenance.
8. Independently regenerate isolated source and require byte or exact geometry/material semantic equivalence. Audit explicit lean package allowlist, every hash and byte size, source/metadata/catalog consistency and relocation. Exclude references/history. Pin manifest approval only when every gate passes.

Heavy commands use coordinated CPU allocation; total production plus reviewers stays at or below8CPU. No external generation, fees, user computer, new Work task or publishing.
